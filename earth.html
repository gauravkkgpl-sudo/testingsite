Green Actions

Practical Earth Preservation Steps

1. Clean Public Spaces

Spend 10 minutes clearing litter from local parks, walking tracks, or streets. Clean surroundings elevate community morale.

2. Plant & Nurture Trees

Plant native saplings during monsoon or adopt existing saplings in your lane, watering them regularly through dry months.

3. Conscious Waste Reduction

Compost organic kitchen waste, segregate plastics, and adopt zero-waste habits before throwing things in municipal bins.

4. Eliminate Single-Use Plastic

Carry your own cloth shopping bags, steel bottles, and utensils to eliminate dependency on disposable plastic items.
