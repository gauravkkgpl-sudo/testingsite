Our Mission

Kindness as a way of living, not an occasional act.

Divya Light began as a simple belief: that everyone has something to give, whether it's an hour of time, a warm meal, a kind word, or the will to clean up a small corner of the world. We exist to make it easy and honest for people to act on that belief — together, at a human scale, without needing to be an institution to make a difference.

Mother Earth

Small habits, kept up together, protect the planet.

Caring for humanity includes caring for the ground beneath us. Here's where to begin.

Clean a public space

Even ten minutes clearing litter from a park or street adds up.

Plant and care for trees

Plant one, or simply water and protect the ones already growing.

Reduce waste

Reuse, compost, and think twice before something goes in the bin.

Avoid unnecessary plastic

Carry your own bag or bottle — a habit that compounds over years.

Divya Light is not a registered NGO, certified charity or non-profit organisation. We are currently two individuals working at a humanitarian level through our own initiative. Contributions are voluntary and are intended to support humanitarian activities and community initiatives. We do not provide or promise any tax benefit or 80G deduction for contributions.

We keep a clear record of contributions received and how they are spent on activities. Initiative funds are not intentionally used for personal expenses. If you can't contribute financially, your time, skills and kindness matter just as much — see the Humanity section for ideas.
